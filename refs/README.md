## Source 1 — TSB16 (44 cubic solids; used for a and B)
F. Tran, J. Stelzl, P. Blaha, "Rungs 1 to 4 of DFT Jacob's ladder: extensive test on the lattice constant, bulk modulus, and cohesive energy of solids", J. Chem. Phys. 144, 204120 (2016), doi:10.1063/1.4948636.
Column `Expt.` of the CMR CSVs. The paper states the experimental values are "corrected for thermal and zero-point vibrational effects". We apply no further correction.
Solids: C Si Ge Sn (diamond); SiC BN BP AlN AlP AlAs GaN GaP GaAs InP InAs InSb (zinc blende); LiH LiF LiCl NaF NaCl MgO (rock salt); Li Na K Rb Cs Ba V Nb Mo Ta W (bcc); Al Ca Sr Ni Cu Rh Pd Ag Ir Pt Au (fcc).

## Source 2 — LEJ14 (26 extra elemental crystals; used for V0 and B)
K. Lejaeghere, V. Van Speybroeck, G. Van Oost, S. Cottenier, "Error estimates for solid-state density-functional theory predictions: an overview by means of the ground-state elemental crystals", Crit. Rev. Solid State Mater. Sci. 39, 1 (2014), doi:10.1080/10408436.2013.772503, arXiv:1204.2733v5.
Supplementary Tab. 2.2: V0,exp = V0,exp0 (room-T or low-T measurement) − ΔV(1) (thermal expansion, 0 K→T) − ΔV(2) (zero-point expansion). Tab. 2.3: B0,exp = B0,exp0 − ΔB(1) − ΔB(2). The per-solid raw value and both corrections are copied into the `correction` field. `build_refs.py` asserts corrected = raw − ΔV1 − ΔV2 for every row.
Structures: the paper's ancillary CIFs (PBE-relaxed shape), scaled isotropically to the experimental volume before the benchmark relaxes shape at constant volume.
Elements: Be B Mg Sc Ti Cr Fe Co Zn Ga As Y Zr Tc Ru Cd In Sb Lu Hf Re Os Tl Pb Bi Po.

## Cross-check
For the 25 elements that appear in both sets the TSB16 and LEJ14 values agree to ≤0.05 % in volume and exactly in B (`crosscheck_TSB16_vs_LEJ14` in solids.json; carbon differs because LEJ14 uses graphite). **The two sources aren't independent:** TSB16 reuses the LEJ14 corrections for elements.

## Excluded on purpose (no honest static-lattice reference for a strongly bound ground state)
* Molecular, rare-gas or van-der-Waals-bound crystals in LEJ14: H, He, N, O, F, Ne, Cl, Ar, Br, Kr, I, Xe, Rn; graphite C; black P; Se and Te (helical chains held together by vdW forces).
* S and Mn: the LEJ14 README states that a simpler non-ground-state structure is used for them.
* Hg: LEJ14 gives no experimental B0 and no zero-point term for V0 (Tab. 2.2/2.3), so it is excluded.
* Transition-metal carbides/nitrides and other compounds from Zhang, Reilly, Tkatchenko, Scheffler, NJP 20, 063020 (2018): their ZPE-corrected experimental values are only in the IOP supplementary file, which returned HTTP 403 to every automated download. **Blocked**, so not used.
* Sol337LC (J. Comput. Chem. 2024): data only available on request, so not used.

## Known limitations of the references
* Oxides: MgO is the only oxide with a ZPE/thermal-corrected B in the sources above. The "oxide" class therefore has n = 1 and is reported per solid, not as a class average.
* Fe, Co, Ni (ferromagnetic) and Cr (antiferromagnetic) are magnetic. LEJ14 itself notes that its simple thermal extrapolation is unreliable for Cr near its Néel temperature.
* Experimental B values carry a typical uncertainty of several % (different techniques: ultrasonic vs. X-ray EOS). a/V0 is far better known (~0.1 %), but the ZPE correction is model-based (Debye/Grüneisen estimates).
* Redistribution: the CMR CSVs and the arXiv ancillary files are public downloads. Check their licences before republishing the raw files. The derived table cites them.
